Sadhana is an iOS app for Carnatic music students and teachers — track your practice, follow your teacher's guidance, and grow your craft one session at a time.

What is Sadhana

A practice companion built for Carnatic learners

Sadhana (साधना) means dedicated practice. Our app brings that spirit into a focused tool that helps students build consistent habits and helps teachers guide their batches — all in one place.

Whether you're learning vocal, veena, mridangam, or flute, Sadhana keeps your sessions structured and your progress visible.

Features

Everything you need to practice well

⏱

Thalam Keeper

Built-in talam cycles with adjustable BPM. Keep your rhythm steady without a separate app.

🎙

Session Recording

Record audio directly in the app and build a library of your progress over time.

📊

Practice Log

See your history at a glance — ragas practiced, duration, and notes over time.

🏫

Batch Collaboration

Teachers post videos, PDFs, and voice notes. Students follow along in their batch feed.

💬

Timestamped Feedback

Comments tied to exact moments in a recording — precise, contextual feedback.

📄

Practice Sheets

Share PDFs in the batch so students always have the latest materials.
