What is Sadhana

A practice companion built for Carnatic learners

Sadhana (साधना) means dedicated practice. Our app brings that spirit into a focused tool that helps students build consistent habits and helps teachers guide their classes — all in one place.

Whether you're learning vocal, veena, mridangam, or flute, Sadhana keeps your sessions structured and your progress visible.

New · Release 1

Every lesson has its own thread

Sadhana is a structured learning platform built around the class. Every lesson has its own thread — the class recording lives there, students post their practice videos in response, and feedback gets pinned to the exact moment it's about. Anyone in the class — student, teacher, or parent — can also start a thread of their own.
