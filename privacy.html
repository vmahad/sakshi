Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Sadhana is developed by Sakshi LLC. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use the Sadhana iOS app and related services.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when using the app:

  • Account information: email address and password used to create your account
  • Practice data: session logs, durations, ragas practiced, and notes you record
  • Audio and media: recordings you make within the app during practice sessions
  • Batch activity: posts, comments, and documents shared within your school batch

How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Operate and maintain the Sadhana app and your account
  • Provide practice tracking, logging, and batch collaboration features
  • Respond to your support requests and communicate with you about the service
  • Improve the reliability and performance of the app

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a cloud database provider. Practice recordings and media files are stored in a private, access-controlled storage bucket. We use industry-standard security practices to protect your information.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Batch and School Data

Content you share within a batch (posts, comments, media) is visible to other members of that batch — your teacher and fellow students. Only users who have been invited to a batch can see its content.

Audio and Microphone Access

Sadhana requests access to your device's microphone solely for the purpose of recording practice sessions within the app. Recordings are stored in your account and are not shared outside the app without your action.

Data Retention and Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at the address below. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
